# ArcPay launch messaging

Country and product figures are deliberately rounded: at least 116 countries were confirmed to have products on
2026-09-21, and that count is known to be an undercount, so the copy says "100+ countries" and "thousands of products".
Attach `arcpay-launch-wide.png` to the first post. X counts every link as 23 characters.

Use `arc-tools.vercel.app/pay` while MetaMask still flags usearckit.locker, so nobody clicks into a red
warning page. Swap the link once the flag is lifted.

## 1. The explainer (Telegram, Discord, a pinned post)

```
Introducing ArcPay 💳

Your USDC on Arc can now buy real things.

ArcPay lets you spend USDC straight from your Arc wallet on gift cards and mobile top-ups from the brands you already use: Steam, PlayStation, Xbox, Netflix, Uber, Airbnb, Starbucks, Nike, Google Play, Apple and thousands more.

How it works
1. Open ArcPay. It detects your country and shows what is popular there, in your local currency.
2. Pick a product and an amount. You see the exact price in USDC before you pay.
3. Pay in one tap. One transaction, no token approval.
4. Your code appears on the page, usually in under a minute, with steps for redeeming it.

Why it is different
• No card, no bank, no KYC form. Just your wallet.
• 100+ countries and thousands of products, from Steam in the US to electricity top-ups in Nigeria.
• The price you see is the price you pay. Conversion costs are included up front.
• If an order cannot be completed after you pay, your USDC is sent back to your wallet automatically.
• Your codes are tied to your wallet. Only you can open them, and they stay in My purchases.

Arc gave us fast, cheap dollars. ArcPay makes them spendable.

Try it: arc-tools.vercel.app/pay

ArcPay is new. Start with a small purchase, and tell us what you want added next.
```

## Notes on what these claims rest on

- "Under a minute": the first real purchase delivered in 13 seconds. Some brands take longer, so the copy says
  "usually".
- "No KYC": ArcPay asks for none. A few individual brands may ask the buyer for details when they redeem.
- "Sent back automatically": true for orders that fail before or during conversion. If a merchant fails after
  being paid, that order is flagged and resolved by hand, so the copy says "cannot be completed", not "guaranteed".
- The supplier behind the catalogue is never named, as requested.
